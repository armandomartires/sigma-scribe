# S18.0 — Arabic, Persian, Urdu, Hebrew: layouts, shaping and right-to-left (v1.8.0)

v1.8 wave W3a (plan §12.6). ✅ DONE (accepted complete 2026-10-27; native review of the machine-drafted strings and layouts deferred); not tagged.

## Scope
- `KeyLayout::{Ar, Fa, Ur, He}`; AltGr is the US layer; compact boards hold letters only.
- `render/src/shape.rs`: `unicode-bidi` runs, one face per stretch, `rustybuzz` shaping, glyphs drawn by index with fontdue; caret stops from the glyph clusters. A row without a right-to-left character never enters this path.
- `TextMeasure` gains `caret_x`, `index_at`, `rtl` (defaults keep every other measure as it was); `wrap` finds the fit by binary search.
- Editor: right-to-left rows are drawn flush right (`row_shift`), caret, tap and selection use the glyph geometry; `Keyboard.rtl` swaps Left / Right; preview shifts right-to-left lines flush right.
- Fonts: Noto Naskh Arabic and Noto Sans Hebrew bundled (plan decision 11 said optional packs; at 176 KB and 27 KB they are not worth one).

## Decisions
- S18.0-D1: digits stay ASCII on all four layouts.
- S18.0-D2: Arabic lam-alef keys type the presentation forms (U+FEFB, FEF5, FEF7, FEF9) because tables hold one character per key; the shaper shows them as ligatures.
- S18.0-D3: Urdu is the phonetic layout; Naskh stands in for Nastaliq (570 KB font; a later pack).
- S18.0-D4: a row's direction is its own first strong letter; paragraph-level direction across wrapped rows is not tracked.
- S18.0-D5: interface mirroring and RTL UI catalogs wait for native review (plan decision 12).
