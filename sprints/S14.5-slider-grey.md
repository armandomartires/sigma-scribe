# S14.5 — v1.4.5: Keyboard size slider on the page, grey while Auto

> **Status:** BUILT 2026-10-22 (v1.4.5, decision 46, not installed, not tagged). User feedback on 1.4.4: no separate background or panel, no focus frame; grey while Auto is on, solid black when off.

**S14.5-D1 — no panel, no frame.** `slider::draw` no longer paints a plate or thresholds its area; it draws on the page colour (`paper` is passed in for the hollow parts). The 2 px focus frame is gone.

**S14.5-D2 — two looks.**
- Auto on (slider disabled): track (dotted), dots (hollow), thumb (hollow) and labels all in `slider::MUTED` = 0x88 grey, on Paper and Night alike.
- Auto off: everything in solid ink, 0x00 on Paper and 0xFF on Night. Moving the thumb changes only the thumb and the bold label, on top of the page colour, so slider moves are DU-safe.

**S14.5-D3 — refresh.** The grey look is static. It appears with the page draw (Settings pages open with the full refresh) and with the Auto toggle, which re-renders the row with the normal partial refresh (`Refresh::Partial` = GC16_FAST in the FBInk shim: full greyscale, non-flashing). That clears the old state completely, so no grey (or black) is left behind. The requested GL16 is not used: GC16_FAST is the existing greyscale partial and does the same job here (see Deviations).

**S14.5-D4 — focus.** Chosen minimal sign: the thumb grows by 3 brief px and its ring doubles in thickness while the slider has focus (only when enabled; a disabled slider ignores arrows).

**Tests:** `crates/render/src/slider.rs` — enabled track/thumb band is only ink + page colour; muted band only grey + page colour; page colour visible at the corners (no plate); focus adds ink but leaves the edges untouched (no frame). `app/tests/keyboard_slider.rs` — the 1.4.4 pure black-and-white assertion is replaced by the enabled/muted band check, in every language, orientation and UI scale; a new test toggles Auto four times on Paper and Night and checks that each state leaves no trace of the other. Behaviour tests unchanged.

**Emulator:** `tests/emu/v1.4.5-slider-grey.script` (Auto on/off on Paper and Night, focus).

**Screenshots** (`docs/screenshots/v1.4.5/` + `eink/`): before (1.4.4) `v1.4.5-before-auto-on`, `v1.4.5-before-auto-off-focus`; after `v1.4.5-auto-on-paper`, `-auto-off-paper`, `-auto-off-focus-paper`, `-auto-on-night`, `-auto-off-night`.

**Deviations:** the Auto toggle uses the existing GC16_FAST partial instead of a dedicated GL16 pass. On Night, "solid black" means the page's ink, which is white.

**On-device checklist (needs approval to install):**
- [ ] Grey slider reads clearly; toggling Auto leaves no ghost.
- [ ] Dragging with Auto off is crisp (DU) with no grey edges left.
- [ ] The focused (bigger) thumb is noticeable.
