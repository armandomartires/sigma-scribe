# S20.1 — Japanese phrases and learning (v1.10.1)

Second part of v1.10 wave W4 (S20.0-D3). ✅ DONE (accepted complete 2026-10-27; native review of the machine-drafted strings and layouts deferred); not tagged.

## Scope
- `ja::phrases`: the best cut is grouped into phrases (a content word takes the particles and endings after it); each has its own candidates (`ja::phrase` for one reading after a boundary move).
- `Composer`: `segs` and the current phrase; Left / Right choose, Shift + arrows move the boundary, Space cycles the current phrase, a tap picks and moves on.
- Editor: the current phrase is underlined heavier when there are two or more.
- `ja::learn`: choices other than the first are remembered for the run.

## Decisions
- S20.1-D1: phrase breaks come from the cut (content after a particle or ending), not from statistics; Shift + arrows fix a wrong break.
- S20.1-D2: learning is in memory only; saving it needs a settings-directory file and is left for a later step.
