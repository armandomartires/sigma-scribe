# S17.0 — Vietnamese typing, Telex and VNI (v1.7.0)

First sub-release of v1.7 wave W2 (plan §12.6: composition). Emulator only; not installed, not tagged.

## Scope
- `KeyLayout::ViTelex` ("vi") and `ViVni` ("vi-vni"): the US key tables; the difference is in the composer.
- `viet.rs`: `convert(keystrokes, vni)`, a pure function from the keystrokes of the current word to the word shown. Marks (â ê ô ă ơ ư đ) and tones (sắc huyền hỏi ngã nặng) can come after the letters they belong to; a repeated key takes it back.
- `Composer::type_char(buffer, layout, c)` replaces `feed` at every typing site (on-screen board, USB, scratch, rename field); it rewrites the buffer from the first changed letter. A moved caret, a selection or a non-letter key starts a new word.

## Decisions
- S17.0-D1: tones by the modern rule (hòa, thủy); the `u` of `qu` and the `i` of `gi` are consonants.
- S17.0-D2: no spell check. Telex can turn English words into tones; use the US layout for them.
- S17.0-D3: Backspace deletes a character as usual (no re-spelling).
- S17.0-D4: no font change; Literata and Atkinson have every Vietnamese letter.

## Tests
`viet.rs` (Telex, VNI, taking keys back), `layouts.rs` (buffer rewriting, caret move), `tests/emu/v1.7.0-vietnamese.script`.
