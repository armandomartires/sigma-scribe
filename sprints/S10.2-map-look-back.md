# S10.2 — v1.0.2: Graph map look, Back navigation

**Status:** BUILT 2026-10-21 (feat/v1.0.2) · **Release:** v1.0.2 · **Requested:** 2026-10-21 23:40 · **Estimate:** 1.5 d · **Depends on:** v1.0.1

## A. Graph map look
- Links are plain straight lines (no arrowheads). Direction stays visible in the List view and the backlinks.
- Unselected notes are small circles: rounded rectangles with radius = height / 2.
- The **selected** note grows horizontally into a pill with its **full** name inside. Selecting is one partial **GL16** refresh (`Refresh::Pointer` class: one merged region, no flash), no animation.
- The current note stays filled (ink circle / ink pill); neighbours of the selection keep a thicker ring and ink links; other notes go quiet.
- **Labels (decision S10.2-D1):** labels sit **beside** the dot (to the right), truncated with "…". They show for the current note and the selection's neighbours always, and for every note only at zoom levels where the average spacing leaves room (the existing `map_labels_all` rule). Otherwise they are hidden: dots only, zoom in to read.
- Tap targets: every node hits within at least a 44 × 44 brief-px square around its centre (more than the dot).

## B. Back navigation
- Session-only history stack of `(note, mode, caret, preview page)`, capped at **50** (oldest dropped).
- Pushed when a note is opened from: a wiki link or backlink in preview, the Graph (Open / second tap, and List rows), To-dos, Contents (jump in the same note: position only), Files.
- A **Back** arrow appears at the far left of the title bar, before Files, only while history is not empty; it restores the previous note, mode (write/preview) and position.
- **Esc** (when no sheet, find bar or selection is open) and **Alt+Left** on a USB keyboard also go back.
- Not done: long-press history list (optional; deferred, decision S10.2-D2).
- Title bar must still fit at 100 % in portrait and landscape (overflow rule from S9.2 applies).
