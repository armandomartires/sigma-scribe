# S20.0 — Japanese typing: romaji, SKK dictionary conversion, candidates (v1.10.0)

v1.10 wave W4, first part (plan §12.6). Emulator only; not installed, not tagged. Needs the CJK font pack on the device.

## Scope
- `KeyLayout::Ja`: US keys, 半 switch left of 1, AltGr = US key; Full board only.
- `ja.rs`: romaji table and feeder, katakana, SKK dictionary loader, cut-by-DP conversion, okurigana stems, candidate list.
- `Composer`: Japanese preedit (reading, pending romaji, candidates), Space convert / cycle, Enter commit, Esc step back, Backspace take back, digits and moved caret end it, `composing()` range for the underline.
- Editor: candidate bar above the keyboard (chips, tap to pick), the text area stops above it; underline under the preedit (and the Korean syllable).
- Fix: USB Backspace no longer ends a composition before acting (it did for Korean in 1.7.1).

## Decisions
- S20.0-D1: usual Japanese-IME flow (Space converts, Enter commits), not SKK's modes: nothing to learn for users of Mozc or MS-IME.
- S20.0-D2: SKK-format dictionaries, seed built in; the full SKK-JISYO.L waits for approval to download (GPL-2.0-or-later, compatible with AGPL-3.0; licence text must ship with it).
- S20.0-D3: one reading is converted at a time, whole; choosing another word inside a phrase and statistics (Mozc) are the next steps.  (done in S20.1)
- S20.0-D4: the 半 key is the mode switch (the 半/全 key on a Japanese keyboard sits where ` is on a US one); AltGr + ` types the backtick.
- S20.0-D5: no JIS kana layout, no Compact board.
