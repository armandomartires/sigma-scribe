# S16.1 — Cyrillic and Greek keyboard layouts (v1.6.1)

Decision 59. Second sub-release of v1.6 wave W1 (plan §12: Latin, Cyrillic, Greek). ✅ DONE (accepted complete 2026-10-27; native review of the machine-drafted strings and layouts deferred); not tagged.

## Scope
- `KeyLayout::Ru` (xkb ru ЙЦУКЕН), `Ua` (xkb ua: ї є і, ґ on BKSL, ₴ on Shift+TLDE) and `Gr` (xkb gr, `;` on Q, ς on W, tonos U+0301 and dialytika U+0308 dead keys on AC10).
- COMPOSE: acute + α ε η ι ο υ ω (and capitals) → ά έ ή ί ό ύ ώ; diaeresis + ι υ → ϊ ϋ. New `decompose()` used by USB typing (replaces the Latin-only search).
- Compact board: Unicode capitals on Shift (was ASCII-only); letter keys in an over-full row share the row width (Cyrillic 12 / 11 / 10 letters).
- FR/PT labels: Russe / Ukrainien / Grec, Russo / Ucraniano / Grego (machine-drafted, native review).

## Decisions
- S16.1-D1: on Ru, Ua and Gr, **AltGr is the whole US layer** (Shift+AltGr = US Shift): Markdown, wiki links and Latin words without a layout switch; no separate "switch language" key. On-screen Alt shows the US board. Ctrl shortcuts use the US letter (existing rule).
- S16.1-D2: **no new font.** Literata covers Greek and Cyrillic (incl. ґ є і ї ₴ №); Atkinson does not, so UI/key labels fall back per character to Literata (decision 47). A sans Cyrillic face (e.g. a Cyrillic Atkinson-like font) can come later if the device review finds the mix ugly.
- S16.1-D3: the Greek compact board has no tonos key (no dead keys on the compact board); use Full or USB for accents.
- S16.1-D4: Ukrainian and Russian Cyrillic ё/ґ sit at the end of the compact bottom row.
- S16.1-D5: UI catalogs for Russian, Ukrainian, Greek (and the other W1 languages) are v1.6.2.
