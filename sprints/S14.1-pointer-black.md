# S14.1 — v1.4.1: pointer always black

> **Status:** ✅ DONE (passed on the Forma, confirmed 2026-10-27); built 2026-10-22 (v1.4.1, decision 42, not tagged).

**Report (user, 1.4.0 on the Forma):** everything works, except the mouse pointer sometimes turns white; it must always be black.

**Cause (from the code; no device access):** `draw_pointer` filled the arrow with the theme's `ink` and outlined it with `paper`.
1. **Night:** ink is 233 and paper 17, so the arrow was near-white with a dark outline, a white arrow by design. This is certain.
2. **Paper:** ink 28 and paper 240 are greys. Pointer moves go out with the **DU** waveform (since 1.0.3), which only drives pixels to black or white; for targets that are neither, the result depends on the waveform table, so 28 can come out white. This is the likely reason for "sometimes" in Paper; only the device can confirm it.
Ruled out in the code: no XOR or inverted draw; the fast path restores the saved under-area and then draws the arrow on top (never the reverse); the GL16 cleanup only runs over the path once the arrow has hidden; FBInk's night-mode / inversion flags are not set; Lucide icons are not used for the pointer.

**Fix (S14.1-D1):** the sprite has only two values, fill 0x00 and outline 0xFF (one cell, 2 px on the Forma), in every theme. **Night keeps the black arrow:** the white outline makes it stand out on the dark page (`docs/screenshots/v1.4.1/v1.4.1-pointer-night.png`), so no theme exception is needed.

**Tests:** `crates/render/src/pointer.rs` (sprite only 0x00 / 0xFF, both themes); `app/tests/pointer.rs` `v141_arrow_is_pure_black_on_every_path` (panel pixels under the sprite equal the sprite after the first move, fast-path moves, over the keyboard, a full render and after the hide cleanup, Paper and Night). Script `tests/emu/v1.4.1-pointer-black.script`.

**Device check:** with the K400, move the arrow over text, the keyboard and the title bar in Paper and Night, and in landscape; it stays solid black and outlined white, and no white arrow appears after it hides and reappears.
