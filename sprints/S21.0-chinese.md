# S21.0 — Chinese pinyin typing: Simplified and Traditional (v1.11.0)

v1.11 wave W4, second part (plan §12.6, decision 13). ✅ DONE (accepted complete 2026-10-27; native review of the machine-drafted strings and layouts deferred); not tagged. Zhuyin follows.

## Scope
- `KeyLayout::ZhHans`, `ZhHant`: US keys, 中 switch left of 1, AltGr = US key; Full board only.
- `zh.rs`: dictionary (seed built in, `dict/pinyin-*.txt` read at first use), syllable segmentation by DFS over all splits, sentence candidate by cheapest cover, word candidates per prefix.
- `Composer`: pinyin preedit with live candidates, Space / 1–9 / tap pick (partial commit keeps the rest), Left / Right choose, punctuation in full width, Enter raw, Esc clear.
- `tools/zh-dict.py` (dictionaries from Rime data), `tools/cjk-pack.py` (GB 2312 + Big5).

## Decisions
- S21.0-D1: live candidates and Space-picks-first (the usual pinyin IME flow), unlike Japanese's convert-with-Space: that is how Chinese IMEs work.
- S21.0-D2: Rime luna-pinyin + essay (LGPL-3.0, approved by the user), words with at least 500 occurrences, 12 per key; Traditional is native, Simplified converted with OpenCC t2s.
- S21.0-D3: first release is pinyin SC + TC; Zhuyin is v1.11.1.
- S21.0-D4: the font pack keeps the whole unified block, 10.6 MB, to keep the cmap valid; one pack for all CJK (Japanese face).
