# S17.1 — Korean typing, Dubeolsik (v1.7.1)

Second sub-release of v1.7 wave W2. Emulator only; not installed, not tagged. Needs the Korean font pack on the device (docs/font-packs.md).

## Scope
- `KeyLayout::Ko` (KS X 5002 Dubeolsik) on the Full and Compact boards and on USB; Shift gives ㅃ ㅉ ㄸ ㄲ ㅆ ㅒ ㅖ; AltGr is the US layer (as ru / ua / gr).
- `hangul.rs`: `compose(jamo)` builds precomposed syllables; compound vowels (ㅘ ㅙ ㅚ ㅝ ㅞ ㅟ ㅢ), compound finals (ㄳ ㄵ ㄶ ㄺ … ㅄ), a final moves to the next syllable on a vowel (a double final splits). `spell` is the inverse, for `usb-type` in the emulator.
- `Composer::backspace`: takes the last jamo back and composes again (한 → 하 → ㅎ).
- Fonts: `Face::Pack`, an optional font file used for characters no bundled face has.

## Decisions
- S17.1-D1: no 한/A key; AltGr types Latin. (Plan §12.7 had one; AltGr matches the other non-Latin layouts.)
- S17.1-D2: the composing syllable is not underlined yet (needs an editor rendering change).
- S17.1-D3: the layout is listed even without a pack; without it Hangul draws as empty boxes. Hiding it when the pack is missing is a follow-up.
- S17.1-D4: Caps Lock does not change jamo.
