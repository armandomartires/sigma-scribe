# S9.2 — Title-bar icons: double size, mockup order

**Release:** v1.0.0 (added before release) · **Estimate:** 1 d · **Depends on:** S9.1

## Goal
(1) Every title-bar icon twice its size — glyphs and tap targets — with the bar growing to fit, in portrait and landscape, Paper and Night, write and preview; every control ≥ 44 px. (2) Icons in exactly the order of `.ai/mockup/kobo-writing-mock.html`.

## Mockup order (read from the HTML/CSS)
`<header class="bar">` is a flex row (`gap: 4px`, `min-height: 52px`, `padding: 6px 8px 6px 14px`). Its children, in order:
1. `button.title-btn` — the file name, `flex: 1` (fills the left; tap to rename).
2. Nine `button.icon-btn` (44×44, 20 px glyphs, stroke 1.75), pushed right by the title:
   **Files · Preview (eye / pencil) · Find and replace · Scratch pad · To-dos · Contents · Graph · Options · Help**.

There is **one group** on the right; **no separators** and no left-side icons. The design brief §3 lists the same order.

## Current order (before S9.2)
Title on the left, then left → right: **Help · Options (Aa) · Graph · Contents · To-dos · Scratch · Find · Preview · Files** — the mockup's order mirrored (the earlier code read the brief's list right-to-left from the edge). Icons 44 brief px (50 px on the Forma), bar 52 brief px (60 px).

## Final order (S9.2)
Title (left, fills) | **Files · Preview · Find · Scratch · To-dos · Contents · Graph · Options · Help** (right group, mockup order, 8 brief px gaps).

- Icons we have that the mockup lacks / vice versa: **none** — all nine match one to one (our Options glyph is "Aa", the mockup's is sliders; kept ours).
- Sheets (Files, Graph, Help, Options) put their close X in the rightmost slot (where Help sits), not on the Files slot, which is now the leftmost icon.
- Preview hides the drawer icons (decision 19), so Scratch / To-dos / Contents leave their slots empty there: icons never move between write and preview. Help is now drawn in preview (it was tappable but invisible before).

## Size and overflow
- Icons 88 brief px (100 px portrait, 134 px landscape), glyphs drawn at twice the scale, close X and active-pane outline doubled too; bar = icon + 2×6 brief px (114 px portrait, 152 px landscape).
- Fit: 9 × 100 + 8 × 9 + 14 = 986 px of 1440 in portrait, leaving 438 px for the title; landscape 1316 of 1920. **No overflow needed on the Forma.**
- Overflow rule (implemented, for narrower panels): keep a title of ≥ 200 brief px; first shrink the gaps to 2 px, then shrink the icons, never below 44 brief px. A "⋯" menu was not needed and was not added (it would hide controls behind an extra tap on e-ink).

## Decisions
- **S9.2-D1** mockup order, one right group, no separators (mockup = brief §3).
- **S9.2-D2** close X in the rightmost slot.
- **S9.2-D3** fixed slots in preview (gaps where drawer icons are hidden).
- **S9.2-D4** overflow by spacing then size (≥ 44); no ⋯ menu.
- **S9.2-D5** the file name keeps its size (the request covered icons only).
