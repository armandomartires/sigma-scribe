# S15.0 — Write panel (v1.5.0)

Plan §12.3–12.4, decisions 49–50. ✅ DONE (accepted complete 2026-10-27). Built and tested with the emulator and CI (decision 48); device checks are in `docs/device-checklist-prerelease.md`.

## What it does
- **Write** button in the status bar (left of Keys) opens the panel; it covers the bottom 45 % (portrait) or 55 % (landscape), on top of the keyboard.
- Panel: **pure white**, a 2-px black rule on top, a dotted black baseline, buttons **Undo**, **Clear**, **Finger/Stylus**, **Cancel**, **Insert** (Insert is the black one). All ≥ 44 px at every UI scale, in both orientations.
- Ink: **pure black**, round discs stamped along each segment (no anti-aliasing), Finger 1.2 mm (14 px) or Stylus 0.6 mm (7 px). Jitter under 2 px is dropped. A stroke that leaves the area runs along its edge; touches outside the panel do nothing while it is open.
- **Undo** removes the last stroke, or brings back a Clear. **Clear** empties the panel (undoable). **Cancel** closes without writing anything.
- **Insert** writes, next to the note, `.ink/<note stem>/ink-NNN.svg` (black round polylines in mm, cropped to the ink, any SVG viewer shows it) and `ink-NNN.strokes.json` (`{"format":"sigma-scribe-ink","version":1,"px_per_mm":11.8110,"pen":…,"strokes":[[[x,y,ms],…],…]}`, panel px, ms since the first point), then puts `![ink](.ink/<stem>/ink-NNN.svg)` on its own line at the caret and autosaves. An empty panel inserts nothing.

## Refresh strategy
| What | Class | Waveform |
|---|---|---|
| Each new segment (merged per input event) | `Refresh::Ink` | DU, never waited for, region = segment + pen width (≥ 16 px) |
| Same with Settings → Fast ink (A2) on | `Refresh::InkA2` | A2 (`fbink_mode` 5 → `WFM_A2`) |
| 600 ms after the last stroke lifts | `PointerCleanup` | one GL16 pass over everything inked since the last cleanup |
| Open / close, Undo, Clear, Pen | `Partial` | GC16_FAST over the changed regions |

Ink frames do not redraw the screen: the segment is drawn straight into the last frame and only its rectangle goes out (same idea as the v1.0.4 pointer frame).

## Settings
Keyboard & input → **Handwriting**: Ink pen Finger / Stylus (`ink_pen=finger`), Fast ink (A2) checkbox (`ink_a2=off`). Older files load with the defaults; older binaries ignore both keys.

## Deviations / deferred (v1.5.0.x)
- Preview shows the ink block as `[image: ink]`; drawing the SVG in preview comes later (own polyline renderer, plan §12.3).
- Renaming or moving a note does not move its `.ink/` folder yet (links break after a rename).
- The strokes file has x, y, t only: the touch event carries no contact size yet (needed for Finger/Stylus Auto later).
- The panel stays white in Night (plan: pure white panel, black ink).
- No recognition (v1.5.1).
