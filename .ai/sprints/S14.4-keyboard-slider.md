# S14.4 — v1.4.4: Keyboard size as Auto + slider

> **Status:** ✅ DONE (passed on the Forma, confirmed 2026-10-27); built 2026-10-22 (v1.4.4, decision 45, not tagged). User request: replace the 1.4.3 Keyboard size choice page with an Auto checkbox and a three-dot slider.

**S14.4-D1 — controls.** Settings → Keyboard & input gets a "Keyboard size" section with two rows: *Auto keyboard size* (checkbox, on by default) and *Keyboard size* (slider: 100% · 125% · 150%). Both fit inline on the page in every language, orientation and UI scale (tested), so there is no separate page.

**S14.4-D2 — behaviour.**
- Tap or drag on the slider snaps to the nearest dot. A touch that starts on the slider owns the finger until it lifts (it may wander off the row; x still picks the dot).
- Tapping or touching the slider gives it focus (a 2 px frame); Left / Right then move it one dot, clamped. Tapping anything else, Esc or leaving the page drops focus.
- While Auto is on the slider is disabled and muted (dotted track, hollow dots, hollow thumb, regular-weight labels) and its thumb shows the effective Auto size (`min(UI scale, 150 %)`). Taps, drags and arrows do nothing.
- Auto off (`Change::KbAuto(false)`) keeps the size Auto gave; then the slider sets it. Auto on goes back to following the UI scale.

**S14.4-D3 — drawing.** Pure 0x00 / 0xFF only: the slider paints its own white (Night: black) plate, and its labels are thresholded, so a DU update shows it exactly. Each stop is a tap target ≥ 44 px (one third of the slider, full row height). The slider row is 84 brief px tall.

**S14.4-D4 — reusable widget.** `crates/render/src/slider.rs` (`Slider`, `geom`, `nearest`, `step`, `draw`) and `options::Kind::Slider`. Other numeric settings could adopt it later (plan decision 45); none are converted in 1.4.4.

**Settings:** `keyboard_size=auto|100|125|150` unchanged; 1.4.3 files load as they were.

**Tests:** `crates/render/src/slider.rs` (snap, step, pure B/W in both states, muted differs), `app/tests/keyboard_slider.rs` (Auto disables; Auto off keeps 125 % at 125 % UI; tap snap; drag snap off-row; arrows clamp; focus lost on other taps; page fit and pure B/W in EN / FR / PT / pseudo-long × portrait / landscape × UI 100–200 % × Auto on/off), `app/tests/keyboard_size.rs` updated.

**Emulator:** `tests/emu/v1.4.4-keyboard-slider.script`; new `press-on NAME` / `move-on NAME` commands; `v1.4.3-keyboard-size.script` now uses the checkbox and slider.
