# Handwriting

Write with a finger or stylus and let Σscribe read it into the note. You can also train the recogniser on your own handwriting.

## Articles

- [The Write panel](the-write-panel.md): Open the Write panel and learn where its keys and writing area are.
- [Modes and word mode](modes-and-word-mode.md): Choose between free writing, letter boxes and sketch, and turn on word mode.
- [Training](training.md): Teach the recogniser your handwriting with the Train key.
- [Gestures](gestures.md): Scratch out, flick left or flick right to edit while you write.
