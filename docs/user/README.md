# Σscribe user guide

For people who use Σscribe on a Kobo. See also the [README](../../README.md) and the [developer documentation](../dev/README.md).

The guide is a help centre: twelve sections, each with short articles, one task per article. Start with the section that matches what you want to do.

## 1. [Set up Σscribe](01-set-up/index.md)

Install Σscribe on the Kobo, keep it up to date, remove it, and get your notes off the device.

- [Install](01-set-up/install.md): Install Σscribe on a Kobo Forma from the GitHub release, with NickelMenu.
- [Update](01-set-up/update.md): Update Σscribe by unpacking a newer package over the old one.
- [Uninstall](01-set-up/uninstall.md): Remove Σscribe from the Kobo, and find out what stays behind.
- [First start](01-set-up/first-start.md): Open Σscribe for the first time and see what the first start sets up.
- [Get your notes off the Kobo](01-set-up/get-notes-off-the-kobo.md): Copy your notes from the Kobo to a computer.

## 2. [Find your way around](02-find-your-way-around/index.md)

A short tour of Σscribe on the Kobo, covering the title bar, the drawer, your files, Settings and quitting.

- [The title bar](02-find-your-way-around/title-bar.md): Use the buttons in the title bar to move around the app.
- [The drawer and its panes](02-find-your-way-around/drawer-and-panes.md): Open the drawer and tick the panes you want beside the note.
- [Files and folders](02-find-your-way-around/files-and-folders.md): Find, make, rename, move and delete notes and folders.
- [Settings overview](02-find-your-way-around/settings-overview.md): Find the Settings categories and what each one holds.
- [Quit and restart](02-find-your-way-around/quit-and-restart.md): Leave Σscribe safely and return to the Kobo normal reader.

## 3. [Write and edit](03-write-and-edit/index.md)

Type into a note and check how it reads.

- [The editor and caret](03-write-and-edit/editor-and-caret.md): Type in a note with the keyboard, and see when it is saved.
- [Preview](03-write-and-edit/preview.md): Switch between Write and Preview to read a note as it looks.
- [Select, copy and paste](03-write-and-edit/select-copy-paste.md): Select text with a touch, a mouse or the keyboard, then cut, copy and paste it.
- [Undo and redo](03-write-and-edit/undo.md): Take back the last change to a note, and put it back again.
- [Line mode and page layout](03-write-and-edit/line-and-paper-modes.md): Set how wide the text runs with Line mode, and tune size, leading and margin.
- [Night and light](03-write-and-edit/night-and-light.md): Turn on Dark mode, set the keyboard tone, and change the front light and warmth.

## 4. [Links and the Graph](04-links-and-graph/index.md)

Link notes to each other with wiki links, complete a link from a list, and see the links on the Graph.

- [Wiki links](04-links-and-graph/wiki-links.md): Link to another note with double square brackets.
- [Link autocomplete](04-links-and-graph/link-autocomplete.md): Pick a note name, or one of its headings, from the bar while you type a link.
- [Backlinks](04-links-and-graph/backlinks.md): See which other notes link to the note you are reading.
- [The Graph](04-links-and-graph/the-graph.md): Open the Graph to see your notes and the links between them.

## 5. [Find, search and replace](05-find-search-replace/index.md)

Find text in the open note, search all your notes, and replace across them.

- [Find in a note](05-find-search-replace/find-in-a-note.md): Find text in the note you have open, and step through the matches.
- [Search all notes](05-find-search-replace/search-all-notes.md): Search every note at once and open a result.
- [Replace in all notes](05-find-search-replace/replace-in-all-notes.md): Replace a word or phrase in every note at once, after checking each change.
- [Undo a replace](05-find-search-replace/undo-a-replace.md): Put your notes back as they were before a replace across notes.

## 6. [Journal](06-journal/index.md)

The journal keeps one note a day, opened from the Calendar pane and filed in folders you choose.

- [Today's note](06-journal/todays-note.md): Open or make today's journal note.
- [The Calendar](06-journal/the-calendar.md): Use the Calendar pane to move between days, months and years.
- [Template](06-journal/template.md): Start every new day note from your own template.
- [Folder layout and UTC offset](06-journal/folder-layout-and-utc-offset.md): Choose how journal notes are filed into folders, and set the UTC offset.

## 7. [Handwriting](07-handwriting/index.md)

Write with a finger or stylus and let Σscribe read it into the note, and train it on your own writing.

- [The Write panel](07-handwriting/the-write-panel.md): Open the Write panel and learn where its keys and writing area are.
- [Modes and word mode](07-handwriting/modes-and-word-mode.md): Choose between free writing, letter boxes and sketch, and turn on word mode.
- [Training](07-handwriting/training.md): Teach the recogniser your handwriting with the Train key.
- [Gestures](07-handwriting/gestures.md): Scratch out, flick left or flick right to edit while you write.

## 8. [Keyboards and languages](08-keyboards-and-languages/index.md)

Type with the on-screen keyboard or a USB keyboard, and use the matching layout for Japanese, Chinese and other scripts.

- [On-screen keyboards and layouts](08-keyboards-and-languages/on-screen-keyboards.md): Show or hide the on-screen keyboard, and find the layouts for other scripts.
- [Japanese input](08-keyboards-and-languages/japanese.md): Type Japanese with the romaji layout.
- [Chinese input](08-keyboards-and-languages/chinese.md): Type Chinese with the pinyin layouts.
- [App language](08-keyboards-and-languages/app-language.md): Choose the language of Σscribe's buttons and menus.
- [Right-to-left text](08-keyboards-and-languages/right-to-left.md): Type Arabic, Persian, Urdu or Hebrew and see how the editor handles it.
- [Font packs](08-keyboards-and-languages/font-packs.md): Install the optional font pack that shows Korean, Japanese and Chinese characters.

## 9. [USB keyboard and mouse](09-usb-keyboard-and-mouse/index.md)

Attach a USB keyboard or mouse to the Kobo, and copy notes off over USB.

- [Connect](09-usb-keyboard-and-mouse/connect.md): Connect a USB keyboard through a powered hub and turn the USB keyboard on.
- [Mouse and touchpad](09-usb-keyboard-and-mouse/mouse-and-touchpad.md): Use a mouse or touchpad to tap, select and scroll.
- [Shortcuts](09-usb-keyboard-and-mouse/shortcuts.md): Keys you can press on a USB keyboard to edit, move and find.
- [Export](09-usb-keyboard-and-mouse/export.md): Copy your notes from the Kobo to a computer over USB.

## 10. [Wi-Fi and sync](10-wifi-and-sync/index.md)

Join a Wi-Fi network from the title bar and keep your notes in step with other devices using Syncthing.

- [Join a network](10-wifi-and-sync/join-a-network.md): Turn Wi-Fi on, join a network and enter its password.
- [Sync with Syncthing](10-wifi-and-sync/sync-with-syncthing.md): Pair the Kobo with a computer or phone and share the notes folder.
- [Conflicts](10-wifi-and-sync/conflicts.md): Find and handle conflict files, and see how deletes spread.
- [Sync status](10-wifi-and-sync/sync-status.md): Read the status rows on the Sync page.

## 11. [Plugins](11-plugins/index.md)

Plugins add panes and screens to Σscribe, and you can switch them on and off, change their settings, and install or remove them.

- [Switch plugins on and off](11-plugins/switch-plugins-on-and-off.md): Switch a plugin on or off and see what changes.
- [Plugin settings](11-plugins/plugin-settings.md): Change a plugin's own settings.
- [Install and remove plugins](11-plugins/install-and-remove.md): Install a plugin from the repository, or remove one you no longer want.

## 12. [Troubleshooting](12-troubleshooting/index.md)

Turn on the log file and read its lines, and find out which features are still experimental.

- [Common problems](12-troubleshooting/common-problems.md): Fixes for the problems people meet most often.
- [The log file](12-troubleshooting/the-log-file.md): Turn on the log file, pick its level and read its lines.
- [Experimental features](12-troubleshooting/experimental-features.md): Turn on experimental features and see which features they include.
- [Report a bug](12-troubleshooting/report-a-bug.md): Tell us what went wrong, with the details that let us find it.
