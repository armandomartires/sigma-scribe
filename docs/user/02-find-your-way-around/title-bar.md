# The title bar

The note's name is at the left. The buttons, left to right:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| Back arrow | **Back**, left of Files. It appears only when there is history to go back through. |
| Folder | **Files**: your notes and folders. New note, New folder, a note's More (Rename, Move, Delete), and the **Trash**, where a deleted note waits until you restore it or empty the Trash. |
| Eye / pencil | Switches between **Write** (the text, with the keyboard) and **Preview** (the note as it reads, in pages you turn with the page buttons). |
| Magnifier | **Find** and replace in the note, or search all your notes ([search.md](../05-find-search-replace/search-all-notes.md)). |
| Panel | The **drawer menu**: tick Contents, To-dos, Scratch or Calendar (the [journal](../06-journal/todays-note.md)) to open that pane beside the note. Drag the drawer's grip to change its width; double tap it to tuck the drawer away. |
| Share symbol | The **Graph**: your notes and the `[[links]]` between them, as a map or a list. |
| Sun | The **front light** menu: Backlight and Warmth. Only on a Kobo with a front light. |
| Wi-Fi | Turns Wi-Fi on and off and joins a network. See [wifi.md](../10-wifi-and-sync/join-a-network.md). |
| Cog | **Settings**. |
