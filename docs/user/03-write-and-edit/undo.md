# Undo and redo

- **Undo:** tap **Ctrl** on the on-screen keyboard, then **Z**. On a USB keyboard press Ctrl+Z.
- **Redo:** Ctrl+Y.
- In the Write panel, the **Undo** and **Redo** keys do the same for typing. After a letter has been read, **Undo** takes back the last thing typed.

## How big a step is

- Typing letters and digits is grouped: one Undo takes back the word you were typing.
- A space, punctuation, a paste, a cut, a line break or a replacement is a step of its own.
- Completing a wiki link from the bar is one step.
- Replace All in Find is one step.

Σscribe keeps the last 200 steps of each open note. A new change after an undo clears what you could have redone.

If there is nothing to undo, Ctrl+Z does nothing.
