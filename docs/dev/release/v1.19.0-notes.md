# Σscribe 1.19.0 for Kobo Forma

Everything since 1.15.0; `CHANGELOG.md` has the details of each step (1.16 to 1.19.0).

## Search across all notes
- **All notes** in the Find bar searches the whole library as you type, on a background thread, and lists the matching lines in place of the page: path and line over the line with the hit in bold, paged like the Graph list. A row opens the note at that line; Back brings the list back. At most 500 matches; Replace stays in This note.

## Journal
- **Calendar pane** in the drawer: one note a day, with day, month and year pickers, Today, and a month view marking the days that have a note. Settings → Journal: folder layout, first day of the week, UTC offset.

## Sync, Wi-Fi and light
- **Sync the notes with Syncthing** (Settings → Sync), with the device ID as a QR code and the fixes that followed on the Forma.
- **A Wi-Fi button** in the title bar: switch, networks, passwords.
- **A front-light button** in the title bar, and Display → Warmth.

## Files, drawer and plugins
- **A Trash in Files:** deleting moves to it; see, restore or empty it. More in Files always shows Rename / Move / Delete.
- **One drawer button** with a menu of panes; close the drawer from its grip, reopen it from the edge, drag its edge to change its width.
- **Install, update and remove plugins** from the plugins repository.
- **Writing panel:** the keys in one block, the rest for writing.

## Fixes (1.18.1)
- `[[note#heading]]` links open the note; Ctrl shortcuts work on the non-Latin on-screen boards; Back never reopens a renamed, moved or deleted note.
