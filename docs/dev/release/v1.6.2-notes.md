# Σscribe 1.6.2 for Kobo Forma

## New
- **Interface in German, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Catalan, Danish and Swedish.** The interface follows your Kobo's language, or choose one in Settings → Language → Interface language.
- The translations are machine-drafted and best effort; About says so. Corrections are welcome.

Includes 1.6.1 (Russian, Ukrainian and Greek layouts), 1.6.0 (eleven more Latin layouts) and 1.5.5 (language override). Your settings carry over unchanged.
