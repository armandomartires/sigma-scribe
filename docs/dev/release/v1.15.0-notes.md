# Σscribe 1.15.0 for Kobo Forma

The first release since 1.0.2. Everything below is new since then; `CHANGELOG.md` has the details of each step (1.0.3 to 1.14).

## Handwriting
- **Write panel:** write anywhere in a blank area; the ink is split into letters and read, with a candidate bar for other readings and keys for punctuation, digits and spaces. Scratch out to erase, flick left or right to delete or add a space.
- **Word mode** (experimental, Settings → Keyboard & input → Handwriting) reads whole words against a word list and the words in your notes. A second handwriting network for the pieces of words cut its character error rate on unseen handwriting from about 27 % to about 10 % (synthetic test fonts).
- **Letter boxes**, one character per box, as an alternative to free writing.
- **Train** the recogniser with samples of your writing, and with **Learn from corrections** (on by default) it keeps learning each time you fix a letter by picking a candidate.
- **Draw:** sketches kept in the note and shown in the preview.

## Languages
- **Interface in 10 languages:** English, French, Portuguese, German, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Catalan, Danish, Swedish. It follows the Kobo's language, or you can choose one in Settings → Language.
- **35 keyboard layouts**, on screen and USB: Latin (US, US International, UK, French, Belgian, German, Spanish, Latin American, Portuguese, Brazilian, Italian, Danish, Norwegian, Swedish/Finnish, Polish, Czech, Romanian, Turkish), Russian, Ukrainian, Greek, Vietnamese (Telex, VNI), Arabic, Persian, Urdu, Hebrew (right to left, shaped), Hindi/Marathi/Nepali, Bengali, Tamil, Thai, Korean, Japanese (romaji, kanji conversion), Chinese Simplified and Traditional (pinyin).
- Korean, Japanese and Chinese characters need a font pack (`fonts/cjk.ttf`, see `docs/font-packs.md`); the Japanese and Chinese dictionaries are included.

## Notes, drawer and Graph
- **Plugins:** the Graph and the drawer's Contents, To-dos and Scratch panes are WebAssembly plugins, **bundled and on by default**. Settings → Plugins switches them on and off, and the Graph has its own settings page (start on Map or List, labels, layout).
- Drawer width is adjustable (Settings → Display → Drawer width). To-dos can be ticked from the drawer.
- Wiki links follow a note when it is renamed or moved.

## Settings and device
- Settings reorganised by category, Kobo-style scroll bars and switches throughout.
- Portrait, landscape or Auto (the Kobo's accelerometer). Backlight control.
- A Kobo-style keyboard as an alternative to the enhanced one; a keyboard size slider.
- Insert / overwrite typing; Home, End, PgUp, PgDn, Ins and Undo keys in the Write panel.
- Optional logging (Settings → Logging) to `.adds/sigma-scribe/log/`.
- Σscribe's own files (notices, dictionaries, ink pages) no longer appear as books in the Kobo library.
- New font stack (harfrust, skrifa, zeno); text looks the same.

Your notes, settings and plugin on/off choices carry over unchanged.
