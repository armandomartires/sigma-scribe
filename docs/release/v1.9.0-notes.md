# Σscribe 1.9.0 for Kobo Forma

## New
- **Hindi / Marathi / Nepali (InScript), Bengali (InScript), Tamil (InScript) and Thai (Kedmanee) typing**: Settings → Keyboard & input → Layout. These use the full on-screen keyboard.
- **Properly shaped text** for Devanagari, Bengali, Tamil and Thai: conjuncts, vowel signs and tone marks sit where they should; the caret and Left / Right move over a whole letter with its signs; Backspace removes one sign at a time.
- The fonts are included; no extra pack is needed.

Includes 1.8.0 (Arabic, Persian, Urdu, Hebrew), 1.7.1 (Korean; needs its font pack), 1.7.0 (Vietnamese) and everything before. Your settings carry over unchanged.
