# Σscribe 1.10.0 for Kobo Forma

## New
- **Japanese typing** (romaji → hiragana → kanji): Settings → Keyboard & input → Layout → Japanese (romaji). Space converts and cycles the candidates, Enter commits, the key left of 1 switches between あ and A. Needs the CJK font pack (`fonts/cjk.ttf`) on the Kobo; see docs/font-packs.md. A small built-in dictionary works out of the box; a full SKK dictionary can be added (docs/japanese.md).
- Korean now underlines the syllable being composed.
- Fix: Backspace on a USB keyboard removes one Korean letter at a time again.

Includes 1.9.0 (Hindi, Marathi, Nepali, Bengali, Tamil, Thai), 1.8.0 (Arabic, Persian, Urdu, Hebrew), 1.7.x (Vietnamese, Korean) and everything before. Your settings carry over unchanged.
