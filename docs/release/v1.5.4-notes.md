# Sigma Scribe 1.5.4 for Kobo Forma

## New
- **Scratch out to delete:** scribble over ink in the Write panel to erase it, or over an empty spot to delete the word you just wrote.
- **Flick left** to delete a character or join a line to the one above; **flick right** for a space.
- **Tap your note** above the panel to move the cursor without closing the panel.

Includes 1.5.3 (new Write panel with free writing), 1.5.2, 1.5.1 and 1.5.0. Your settings carry over unchanged.
