# Sigma Scribe 1.5.5 for Kobo Forma

## New
- **Choose the app's language yourself:** Settings → Language → **Override system language**. Off (the default), Sigma Scribe follows your Kobo's language. On, pick English, Français or Português for the app only; your Kobo's own language is not changed.

Includes 1.5.4 (handwriting gestures), 1.5.3 (new Write panel), 1.5.2, 1.5.1 and 1.5.0. Your settings carry over unchanged.
