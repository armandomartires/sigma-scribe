# Sigma Scribe 1.5.3 for Kobo Forma

## New
- **A new Write panel:** suggestions across the top, a small block of keys on the left (Draw, backspace, return, punctuation, 123, language, space) and a big blank area to write in.
- **Write freely:** no more boxes. Write a word or a few letters anywhere in the area; when you pause, it is typed into your note with a space. Tap a suggestion to fix it, ‹ › for more.
- **123** for numbers, the **globe** to read only English, French or Portuguese letters.
- **Word mode (experimental):** off by default. Reads whole words against a word list; try it if you join your letters.
- The 1.5.2 letter boxes are still there: Settings → Keyboard & input → Handwriting → Letter boxes.

Includes 1.5.2 (letters, training, wiki links follow renames), 1.5.1 and 1.5.0. Your settings carry over unchanged.
