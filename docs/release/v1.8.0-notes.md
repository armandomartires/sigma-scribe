# Σscribe 1.8.0 for Kobo Forma

## New
- **Arabic, Persian, Urdu and Hebrew typing** on the on-screen keyboard and USB keyboards: Settings → Keyboard & input → Layout.
- **Right-to-left text**: joined Arabic-script letters, Hebrew, mixed with Latin and numbers; rows sit flush right; the caret and Left / Right follow the reading direction.
- The fonts (Noto Naskh Arabic, Noto Sans Hebrew) are included; no extra pack is needed.

Includes 1.7.1 (Korean; needs its font pack), 1.7.0 (Vietnamese) and everything before. Your settings carry over unchanged.
