# Sigma Scribe 1.5.2 for Kobo Forma

## New
- **Letters:** in the Write panel, tap **Letters** and write one character per box with a finger or a stylus. Each letter is typed into your note. Five guesses appear after each one; tap another to fix it. Space, Erase and Return are on the panel. English, French and Portuguese letters with accents, digits and punctuation.
- **Train it on your hand (optional):** Letters → Train shows one character at a time; write it and Save. Your samples stay on the Kobo (`notes/.handwriting/samples.txt`) and the app adapts to them. Settings → Keyboard & input → Handwriting → My handwriting → Delete removes them.
- Recognition is fully offline, with the app's own small model, trained only on computer-generated ink (no one's real handwriting).

## Fixes
- **Renaming or moving a note keeps the links to it:** `[[links]]` in your other notes and the scratch pad are updated, including `[[note|label]]` and `[[note#section]]`.

Includes 1.5.1 (ink in preview), 1.5.0 (Write panel) and 1.4.3–1.4.6. Your settings carry over unchanged.
