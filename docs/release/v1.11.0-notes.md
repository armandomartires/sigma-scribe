# Sigma Scribe 1.11.0 for Kobo Forma

## New
- **Chinese typing, pinyin** (Simplified and Traditional): Settings → Keyboard & input → Layout → Chinese Simplified (pinyin) or Chinese Traditional (pinyin). Type pinyin and pick from the candidate bar: Space writes the first, 1–9 or a tap pick another, Left / Right move the choice; the best whole-sentence guess comes first. Punctuation is written in full width. Needs the CJK font pack (`fonts/cjk.ttf`) and, for the full vocabulary, the dictionaries in `dict/` (included in the package); see docs/chinese.md.
- Japanese: conversion by phrase (1.10.1) and the full SKK dictionary (1.10.0).

Includes 1.10.x (Japanese), 1.9.0 (Hindi, Marathi, Nepali, Bengali, Tamil, Thai), 1.8.0 (Arabic, Persian, Urdu, Hebrew), 1.7.x (Vietnamese, Korean) and everything before. Your settings carry over unchanged.
