Σscribe

A distraction-free Markdown notebook for the Kobo Forma. Native Rust, plain .md files, typed or handwritten.

What it does

Writing

  • On-screen keyboard, Compact or Full, with a size slider and Split, Full or Centre in landscape.
  • Touch selection, cut, copy, paste, undo, redo, autosave.
  • Find and Replace, line mode, size, leading, margin, tabs.

Notes and links

  • A library of notes and folders, with a Trash you can restore from. Notes are plain Markdown on the Kobo drive.
  • Markdown preview in pages.
  • Wiki links [[note]] with backlinks; renaming a note updates the links to it.
  • A side drawer with Contents, To-dos across all notes and a Scratch pad, and a Graph of how notes connect.

Handwriting

  • Write anywhere in a blank area; the ink is split into letters and read, with a candidate bar for other readings.
  • Word mode and fast ink (experimental), letter boxes, training on your own writing, and sketches kept in the note.

Languages

  • Interface in 10 languages: English, French, Portuguese, German, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Catalan, Danish and Swedish.
  • 35 keyboard layouts, on screen and on a USB keyboard: Latin, Cyrillic, Greek, Vietnamese, Arabic, Persian, Urdu, Hebrew, Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Thai, Korean, Japanese and Chinese, among others.
  • Korean, Japanese, Chinese, Arabic, Persian, Urdu, Hebrew, Hindi, Marathi, Nepali, Bengali, Tamil and Thai are experimental: they appear in the layout list only with Settings → Experimental features on.
  • Korean, Japanese and Chinese characters need a font pack; the dictionaries are included.

Device

  • Paper or Night, Literata or Atkinson Hyperlegible, portrait and landscape, backlight control.
  • A Wi-Fi button in the title bar to turn the radio on and off and join networks.
  • USB keyboard and mouse over USB-OTG through a powered hub or powered cable.

Plugins

  • WebAssembly plugins in a sandbox, switched on and off in Settings, each with its own settings page. See the plugins page.

Sync

  • Optional Syncthing keeps the notes folder in step with your computers while Σscribe is open. No account, no server of ours. Off by default.

Install in three steps

  1. Install NickelMenu on the Kobo from its own release.
  2. Download sigma-scribe-<ver>-kobo.tar.gz from the latest release and unpack it onto the root of the Kobo drive over USB.
  3. Eject. Σscribe is now in the NickelMenu. Your notes live in sigma-scribe/notes on the drive, and an update keeps them.

Details, checksums, update and uninstall are in the install guide.

Tested on the Kobo Forma, firmware 4.38.x (4.38.23697). Other Kobo models are untested. Firmware 5.x is not supported.
