# Changelog

All notable changes to Σscribe. Versions are tagged after they pass on the Kobo Forma.

## Unreleased

- **A Trash in Files: see what you deleted, restore it, or empty it.** While the trash holds something, Files has a third button beside New note and New folder: **Trash (n)**. It opens the Trash, a list of what Delete moved there, newest first, with each note's name, the folder it was in and when it was deleted ("in drafts · 2 h ago", "Yesterday", "3 days ago"). Tap a note to select it, then **Restore**: it goes back to the path it had, with its folders made again and its ink; if something now has that name, it comes back as `name (restored).md` (`(restored 2)` and so on) and nothing is overwritten. A deleted folder is one row and comes back whole. **Empty trash** takes two taps (the second reads "Tap again to empty") and removes everything under `.trash` for good, the only delete that cannot be undone. Restoring the last item, or emptying, returns to Files, and the Trash button goes. A restored note is in Files, Find and the to-do list at once. Each trash folder now holds an `.origin` file naming the path it had, so a folder holding one folder is not mistaken for a note in a folder; trash made by the previous build (no `.origin`) is still listed and restored as well as its layout allows. New `Library::list_trash`, `trash_len`, `restore`, `empty_trash`, `Trashed`; 15 new strings in the nine catalogs (Trash, Restore, Empty trash, the two-tap and status lines, "in {name}" and the relative times); emulator step `age-trash`, controls `Trash`, `Restore`, `Empty trash`; script `tests/emu/v1.17-trash.script`, screenshots in `docs/screenshots/v1.17/`. Tests: `crates/core/src/library.rs` (list, restore with name clash and nested folders, folder with one subfolder, ink, older trash, empty), `crates/render/src/files.rs`, `app/tests/trash.rs`.
- **Display → Warmth: a warm-to-cool slider for the front light.** Eleven stops under Backlight, Cool on the left to Warm on the right, shown only when the device has the Forma's mixer file `/sys/class/backlight/tlc5947_bl/color`. It does what KOReader does for the Forma ("Frost"): the file takes 0 to 10 and is inverted, so warmth step *n* writes `10 - n` (KOReader `frontend/device/kobo/device.lua`, `KoboFrost`: `nl_min = 0`, `nl_max = 10`, `nl_inverted = true`; `frontend/device/sysfs_light.lua`, `setNaturalBrightness`); the mixer works whether or not the white light is on. `tlc5947_bl/brightness` (0 to 4096) is not touched. Unlike Backlight, the step **is saved** (`warmth=` in `.sigma-settings`, `keep` until you move the slider) and written to the mixer at start, because the stock reader never reads the mixer back; with nothing saved the light is left as it is and the slider shows what the file reads, else Cool. New `sigma_device::light::Warmth`, `Device::has_warmth` / `warmth_step` / `set_warmth`, `Change::Warmth`, `Settings::warmth`; three new strings (Warmth, Warm, Cool) in the nine catalogs; emulator steps `warmth N [unreadable]` and `expect-warmth`. **Not yet tried on the Forma**: whether the file reads back, and which end looks orange (`.ai/device-pass-s7.md` §7.4). Tests: `crates/device/src/light.rs`, `app/tests/warmth.rs`, `tests/emu/v1.17-warmth.script` (screenshots in `docs/screenshots/v1.17/`).

- **Close the drawer from its grip, reopen it from the edge.** A double tap on the drawer's grip (or a double click with a mouse or touchpad), or dragging the grip to the screen edge, closes the whole drawer. A tab stays where the drawer's outer edge was (on the right, left of the scroll bar; on the left, at the screen edge); tap it to bring back the same panes (and the scratch focus), or drag it in to reopen at the width where the finger lifts. Ticking a pane in the drawer menu forgets what the grip closed. The tab sits over that bit of the scroll bar; the rest of the bar works as before. What the grip closed is not kept across a restart. The grip (and the tab) now has rounded ends, an outline as wide as the scroll bar thumb's round a 50 % grey fill, and three white dots down its middle; the drawer's edge line runs into its ends with no white gap. The scroll bar keeps its strip at the right of the page: a drawer on the right now ends left of it, as far from the bar as the bar is from the screen edge, instead of covering it, in write mode and in preview (a drawer on the left never met it; the bar is on the right in every language). Emulator control `DrawerTab`. Tests in `app/tests/drawer_handle.rs`, `app/tests/drawer_menu.rs`, `app/tests/pointer.rs`.

- **One drawer button instead of three icons.** The title bar has a single drawer button (a panel on the drawer's side) where the Contents, To-dos and Scratch icons were. A tap opens a small menu under it with a checkbox, icon and name for each pane; tap rows to show or hide panes, the menu stays open for several. The button, Escape or a tap anywhere else closes it, and that tap does nothing else (no typing, no caret move). The button is shaded while a pane or the menu is open. Only panes whose plugin is on are listed; with none, the button goes. Works in write mode and preview. No new strings (the pane names were translated). New `sigma_render::drawer_menu` (`MenuHit`, `drawer_menu_rows`, `draw_drawer_menu`), `Hit::DrawerMenu` replaces `Hit::Drawer`, Lucide `panel-right` and `panel-left`; emulator control `Panels` and step `pane NAME`; `App::control_steps`. Tests: `crates/render/src/drawer_menu.rs`, `app/tests/drawer_menu.rs`.

- **Check for plugins works without `curl`.** On the Forma, Check for plugins failed with "No such file or directory": the Kobo has no `curl`. The app now downloads itself (ureq with rustls and the bundled Mozilla root certificates), https only, redirects included, with the same size and sha256 checks. The launcher no longer stops `dhcpcd`, so Wi-Fi turned on in the Kobo's reader stays up while Σscribe runs, and it logs the network it finds (`net:` lines in `sigma-scribe.log`). Tried on the Forma: the index was fetched and a plugin installed from it. New dependency `ureq` (rustls, ring, webpki-roots); ISC and CDLA-Permissive-2.0 added to the licence allow list. Ignored test `live_repository` fetches the real index and a package.

- **Remove a plugin.** Settings → Plugins has a Remove row under each plugin; it is greyed while the plugin is on, and removing deletes its folder and its `plugins.state` line. One new string ("Remove") in the nine catalogs; the "Turn the plugin off first" note is English only, like "Turn the plugin on first".

- **Word mode hint corrected.** Settings → Handwriting → Word mode said "off is more accurate", which stopped being true when word mode got its own piece model (1.15.0). It now says it is best for joined-up writing, in all ten languages.
- **Install and update plugins from the plugins repository.** Settings → Plugins → Repository → Check for plugins lists what the repository offers, with Install and Update buttons. The Kobo needs Wi-Fi on; the download uses the device's `curl` (not tried on the Forma yet), over https, from the plugins repository only. Each package is checked against the size and sha256 in the index before it is unpacked, installed in one step so a failure keeps the old version, and an update keeps your on/off choice and the plugin's settings. An index older than the last one is refused. The index is not signed yet. New `sigma_core::pluginpkg` (sha256, package reading, `install_in`, `uninstall_in`), `Change::PluginCheck` and `PluginGet`; six new strings in the nine catalogs; `scripts/make-index.sh` in the plugins repository builds the packages and index. Removing a plugin still means deleting its folder. Tests: `crates/core/src/pluginpkg.rs`, `app/tests/plugin_repo.rs`.
- **Drag the drawer's edge to change its width.** While a drawer pane is open, a small grip (a short black bar in a white halo, a finger wide) sits on the drawer's inner edge, in the middle of the page. Put a finger on it and slide: the stop of Settings → Display → Drawer width nearest the finger applies when you lift (one redraw), and the choice is saved like the slider's. A touch that does not start on the grip does what it did. New `EditorScreen::drawer_handle`, `draw_drawer_handle`, `drawer_stop_at`, `drawer_width_at`; emulator control `DrawerHandle`; tests in `app/tests/drawer_handle.rs`.
- **Preview layout time is logged and measured.** Entering preview logs `preview layout of <note> (<bytes> bytes): <n> ms` at Debug level (Settings → Logging). New ignored test `app/tests/preview_perf.rs` and a table in `docs/perf.md`: about 0.65 ms per KB on the desktop (100 KB in 60 ms, 500 KB in 337 ms). No change in behaviour.
- **Deleting a note or folder moves it to a trash.** Files → Delete (second tap) now moves the item to `.trash/<unix seconds>/<its path>` in the notes folder, with a note's ink beside it, instead of removing it. Nothing in the trash is listed, searched or indexed, and the last note still cannot be deleted. (The Trash in Files, below, lists it, restores it and empties it.) New `Library::trash`; test `trash_moves_instead_of_deleting`.
- **Backlight device confirmed on the Forma.** The Forma has two backlight devices, `mxc_msp430.0` (0 to 100) and `tlc5947_bl` (0 to 4096). The app drives `mxc_msp430.0`, and Display → Backlight changes the brightness there (device log and a hands-on check, 2026-10-27). The earlier "not yet tried" note no longer applies. The warm/cool slider is Display → Warmth, above.

## 1.15.0 — 2026-10-27 (tagged; release built by CI; not yet tested on the Forma as a whole)
- **The package bundles the Graph and the drawer panes.** `graph-map`, `contents-pane`, `todo-pane` and `scratch-pane` are in `.adds/sigma-scribe/plugins/` (built from the plugins repository, commit in each `SOURCE.txt`). A new install turns them on: `launch.sh` writes `plugins.state` only when there is none, and the package has none, so an update keeps your on/off choices. Before, a fresh install had no Graph and no drawer panes. `check-package.sh` requires them and refuses a `plugins.state`.
- **README:** current screenshots (`docs/screenshots/v1.15/`, `tests/emu/v1.15-readme.script`) and the full feature list.
- Version 1.15.0: 1.12 to 1.14 were already used as labels of builds installed during the device passes.
- **Fix: a Help page opened from Settings was blank.** Settings → Help & about → App info, Markdown reference and USB keyboard, mouse & export opened Help on the right page but never laid its text out, so the page showed only its title until Back and a second open. Every way into a Help page now lays it out (`App::show_help_page`). Help also logs each page it opens, and the Kobo logs every full refresh and what follows one within 1.5 s (Debug level). Test: `app/tests/help_from_settings.rs`.
- **Code: one table per setting** (no change in behaviour; the settings file is byte for byte the same). `settings::FIELDS` lists every line of `.sigma-settings` with how it is read and written, replacing the hand-written 34-key `parse` and `serialize`; a new `Words` trait gives each settings enum (paper, type, keyboard tone, drawer side, orientation, rotation, landscape keys, pad, ink pen) one list of its values with the file's word and the Settings label, and the Settings rows build their cells from it. Tests: the file against the old output, every field away from its default round-tripping, keys listed once, every value's word.
- **Code: `app/src/lib.rs` is split by concern** (no change in behaviour). It was 5,537 lines; `App`, its state and its start-up stay there (about 700 lines) and its methods move to eighteen child modules, each with its own `impl App` block as `plugin_pane.rs` and `plugin_screen.rs` already had: `events`, `render`, `settings`, `drawer`, `files`, `ink`, `editor`, `keyboard`, `pointer`, `rotation`, `paged_bar`, `preview`, `find`, `graph`, `nav` (links and history), `help`, `splash`, `script` (the emulator and test hooks). An edit to one screen no longer rebuilds one 5,500-line file, and branches stop colliding on it.
- **Handwriting learns from your corrections.** When you fix a letter by picking a candidate (Letter boxes, or an alternative in the free area that changes one letter), that letter's ink is kept as a sample of your writing in `.handwriting/learned.txt` next to the Train samples, and the recogniser is fitted again in the background. At most 3 per character, the newest replacing the oldest, so a mistaken pick soon ages out; picking again for the same reading replaces what it taught. Readings you accept, word-list alternatives and multi-letter changes teach nothing. New switch: Settings → Keyboard & input → Handwriting → **Learn from corrections** (on by default; `ink_learn=`); Delete samples removes the learned ones too. One new string pair in all nine catalogs. Also fixed: an enrolled sample of the character `#` was ignored when the samples file was read.
- **Word mode reads much better**, with a second handwriting network for the pieces of words (`assets/hwr/pieces.hwr`, `hwr-train train-pieces`): trained also on pieces cut from synthetic words, with a "not a letter" class for half letters and letter pairs. Word CER after enrolment: Handlee 14.0 % → 8.2 %, Delius 24.3 % → 9.5 %, training fonts 14.3 % → 7.4 %; touching letters in the free area 34.6/37.4 % → 32.4/32.6 %. The word-mode gate (≤ 15 %) now passes on the synthetic fonts. Letters and printed free writing keep the letter model and do not change. The app is 140 KB larger; fine-tuning on your samples (in the background) takes about twice as long.
- **Word mode reads better** (Settings → Handwriting → Word mode, and touching letters in the free area). Character error rate on the held-out handwriting fonts after enrolment: Handlee 24.1 % → 14.0 %, Delius 27.2 % → 24.3 %; training fonts 15.3 % → 14.3 %; touching letters in the free area 36.7/41.3 % → 34.6/37.4 %. Enrolment now helps word reading instead of hurting it: the word decoder no longer uses the nearest-sample boost, which also lifted half letters. A letter-width prior (`assets/hwr/widths.txt`, `hwr-train widths`) and a per-letter bonus of 7 (was 8). Letters and printed free writing are unchanged. The word-mode gate (≤ 15 %) still fails on Delius; see `.ai/sprints/S15.3-word-mode.md`.
- **Handwriting model retrained** on letters drawn with the new font stack (`cargo run -p hwr-train --release -- train`, unchanged settings and seeds; on the old stack the same command reproduces the old model byte for byte). Single characters are read better: 84.8 % top-1 on the two held-out fonts (was 82.5 %), 97.3 % on new jitter of the training fonts (was 97.4 %), and enrolment helps more. Whole words are mixed: Handlee about the same or better, Delius without enrolment worse (63 % of words exact, was 70 %).
- **New font stack.** Text is now shaped with `harfrust` (the HarfBuzz project's Rust port, replacing `rustybuzz`), fonts are read with `skrifa` and glyphs drawn with `zeno` (replacing `fontdue` and its `ttf-parser`). The old crates are no longer maintained. Text looks the same: across all 310 emulator screenshots no text moved or reflowed, and Arabic, Hebrew, Indic and Thai shaping is unchanged; only anti-aliasing differs by a few grey levels at letter edges. `cargo deny` now passes with no exceptions; `Unicode-3.0` (yoke, zerofrom) is added to the licence allow list. `THIRD-PARTY.txt` lists the new libraries. New `sigma_render::typeface::FontFile`, which `hwr-train` now uses too.
- **Development:** new `deny` job in CI runs `cargo deny check` (`deny.toml`): RustSec advisories, licences (permissive, AGPL-compatible allow list), banned crates and sources, for the desktop and Kobo targets. `rustybuzz` and `ttf-parser` are flagged as unmaintained and listed as known exceptions. No change to the app.

The locked dependencies already needed it: `wasmparser` 0.262, used by `wasmi` 2, requires 1.88. With the higher minimum, clippy suggests `is_multiple_of` and `as_chunks`; the five places it flagged now use them (same behaviour). CI checks the minimum in a new `msrv` job and now also builds the docs with warnings as errors. New `CONTRIBUTING.md` (layout, targets, checks, conventions) and a short project `CLAUDE.md`. `sigma-plugin-api` and `sigma-plugin-host` are fully documented, with doctests; `sigma_plugin_api::DecodeError` now implements `Display` and `std::error::Error`. No change to the app.
