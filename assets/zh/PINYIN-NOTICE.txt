Pinyin dictionaries (dict/pinyin-hans.txt, dict/pinyin-hant.txt)
================================================================

Built by tools/zh-dict.py from the Rime project's data:

  rime-luna-pinyin   luna_pinyin.dict.yaml   (Luna Pinyin, 明月拼音)
  rime-essay         essay.txt               (word frequencies)

Luna Pinyin is itself refined data from Chewing / 新酷音 (LGPL), OpenCC
(Apache-2.0), the Android Pinyin IME (Apache-2.0), CC-CEDICT and
MoeDict / 萌典 (CC0 1.0). Sources:
  https://github.com/rime/rime-luna-pinyin
  https://github.com/rime/rime-essay

The Simplified file is the Traditional data converted with OpenCC (t2s,
Apache-2.0). The files are modified from the originals: only words seen at
least 500 times in essay.txt, at most 12 words per pinyin key, weights are
the essay frequencies (times the reading's share for characters with several
readings).
